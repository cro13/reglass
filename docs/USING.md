# Using ReGlass

Once your glasses are set up and your Anthropic key passes its test, you can talk to ReGlass or type on your
iPhone. [Getting started](GETTING-STARTED.md)

## Talk or type

- Say “Hey Meta” and ask your question. Or touch and hold the glasses’ touchpad, wait for the chime, then speak.
- After a reply, listen for the two-note chime. You have a few seconds to ask a follow-up without saying “Hey Meta.”
- Say “Hey Meta” while a reply is playing to interrupt it and ask something else. Say “Hey Meta, stop” to end it.
- In **Chats**, type in **Ask ReGlass** and tap the up arrow to start a chat. Open a chat and use **Reply to ReGlass**
  to continue it. Typed replies appear on screen and are read aloud when iPhone audio is routed to the glasses or
  headphones. Tap **Stop** to interrupt one.
- To hear a typed answer through the iPhone speaker, use **Settings → Assistant → Talk to ReGlass**. Type your
  request and tap **Ask**.

Use the gear at the top right of **Chats**, **Notes** or **Reminders** to open **Settings**.
[Voice and speech](VOICE.md)

## Find and organise your library

| Tab | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Chats** | Open a conversation to read what you asked and what ReGlass replied. Voice and typed requests appear here. |
| **Notes** | Say “Take a note” followed by the note, or type in **Write a note** and tap the up arrow. Open a note to edit its title or text. |
| **Reminders** | Ask for a reminder with a time, such as “Remind me tomorrow at nine to call the dentist.” You can also type it in the bar at the bottom. Ask to add items to a list, such as “Add oat milk to my shopping list.” Lists are in this tab too. Tap an item to tick it off. |
| **Search** | Find words across chats, notes, reminders and list items. Tap a result to open the match. |

Use the category chips in **Notes** and **Reminders** to filter by categories such as **Work** or **Shopping**.
Touch and hold a note, reminder or list item and choose **Move To** to change its category. To start a category,
name it when you ask ReGlass to save or move something.

Deleted items stay in **Recently Deleted** for 30 days. Open it at the bottom of the relevant tab when it has
deleted items. Tap an item and choose **Put Back** to restore it. Search can also find matches in Recently Deleted.

## Connectors

Open **Settings → Assistant → Connectors** to switch features on or off. Tap a connector for examples and options.
Use the permission button when one appears. [Connector permissions and examples](CONNECTORS.md)

| Connector | What it can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Timers** | Start, check or cancel countdowns that ring on the connected glasses. |
| **Reminders** | Set, list, change, cancel or snooze ReGlass reminders. |
| **Lists** | Add, read and tick off items in ReGlass lists. |
| **Notes** | Save, find and add to notes. Move items between categories and restore deleted notes, reminders or list items by voice. |
| **Calendar** | Read events, find free time and add an event after you confirm. |
| **Alarms** | Set, check, cancel and snooze iPhone alarms. |
| **Apple Music** | Start or control playback and ask what's playing. Full catalogue search needs MusicKit and an Apple Music subscription. |
| **Weather** | Get local forecasts or ask about a named place. Set a home city in the connector's options if you prefer. |
| **Interpreter** | Start the experimental live interpreter by voice. |

Claude's web search is available without a connector switch.

## Assistant settings

Open the **Assistant** section in **Settings**.

- **Keys**. Add, test, replace or remove your Anthropic and ElevenLabs keys.
- **Model**. Choose Claude Haiku 5.5 (the default), Sonnet 5.5, Opus 5.5 or Fable 5.2. The choice applies from the
  next conversation.
- **Voice and speech**. Choose the voice that reads replies and the service that recognises your speech. Apple
  on-device voice and recognition are the defaults. Add an ElevenLabs key to use an ElevenLabs voice or
  ElevenLabs Scribe. [Voice and speech](VOICE.md)

## Wake word

In **Settings → Wake word**, check what set is on your glasses and turn **Listen for “Hey Meta”** on or off. You
can still touch and hold the touchpad when listening for the wake word is off.

Under **Wake word sets**, download the factory set or use **Import a Set** to select one from Files. Installing a
set on the glasses is a separate step. [Wake word files and installation](WAKE-WORD.md)

## Firmware updates

Open **Settings → Firmware** or tap **View** on the update banner in **Chats**. On supported Audio glasses, tap
**Update Glasses**, then **Install** when an update is offered.

Charge the glasses to at least 60% beforehand. Keep them off the charger, near your iPhone, and leave ReGlass open.
Allow about 20 minutes. The glasses restart and ReGlass checks the new version. [Firmware updates](FIRMWARE.md)

## Live interpreter

The experimental interpreter translates the other person's speech into English and plays it through your glasses.
Connect the glasses, add Claude and ElevenLabs keys, and choose **ElevenLabs Scribe** in **Voice and speech**.

Open **Settings → Assistant → Live interpreter**, choose **Their language** and tap **Start**. With **Real time**
off, say “Hey Meta” after tapping **Start**. To start by voice directly, enable **Interpreter** in **Connectors**
and say “Hey Meta, interpreter, French.” Tap **Stop** or say “stop interpreting” when you're done.
[Live interpreter](INTERPRETER.md)

## Return to Meta AI

In ReGlass **Settings**, switch off **Stay connected** or tap **Forget These Glasses** and confirm. Open Meta AI and
set up the glasses there again to return ownership to Meta AI.

To switch back later, close Meta AI and tap **Reconnect or Set Up Again** in ReGlass settings. If you forgot the
glasses, tap **Set Up** on the card in **Chats**. [Ownership and switching apps](OWNERSHIP.md#going-back-to-meta-ai)

## Developer settings and activity log

Open **Settings → Developer** to find the glasses' model code and serial number. Tap **Version** five times to
show **Activity log**. It stays visible after you close the app.

Use **Filter** to find an event, or **Export** to share the full log. Before posting publicly, copy the relevant
lines and remove device identifiers and personal details. [Read and share the activity log](TROUBLESHOOTING.md#read-and-share-the-activity-log)
